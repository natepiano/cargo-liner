- [ ] **Decide what counts as proof of integration, so an amended commit does not re-arm a released reservation**
  - Target: `cargo-berth` engine — `crates/cargo-berth/src/reservation/{evidence.rs,lifecycle.rs}`, `crates/cargo-berth/src/board/mod.rs`, and `crates/cargo-berth/src/board/tests.rs`.
  - Why needed: integration proof is commit identity rather than content. `reservation/evidence.rs:78` demotes a `Proven` reservation to `TrunkRewritten` once its protected tip stops being an ancestor of trunk, so `git commit --amend` evaporates the proof even though every changed line is still on trunk; `/validate_and_push` amends by design, folding cargo-mend and rustfmt results into the last commit, so it invalidates a release on essentially every push. `reservation/lifecycle.rs:153` then maps `NotIntegrated | TrunkRewritten | ObjectUnknown` to `EditBlockingStatus::Blocking`, returning a reservation released with a terminal disposition to blocking. The board therefore reports `stage: released` and `edit_blocking_status: blocking` together, which reads as a contradiction and was misdiagnosed as one during the 2026-08-26 same-worktree incident. Worktree-identity edit blocking makes both harmless for single-worktree work, because the re-blocked reservation is then always the caller's own; they still bite across worktrees after a rebase.
  - Completion condition: a rewritten trunk consults path-level reachability — are the reservation's scopes present on trunk — before demoting proof or re-arming the block; a reservation whose content reached trunk under an amended or rebased commit stays proven and stays non-blocking; `stage: released` with `edit_blocking_status: blocking` is either impossible or carries a rendered explanation naming the rewrite; `board/tests.rs:435` `assert_trunk_rewritten_action` is revised rather than left asserting the old shape; and the cargo-berth test, board, drift, and lint gates stay green.
